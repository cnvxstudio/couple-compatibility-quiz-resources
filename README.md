## What makes a useful couple quiz?

Choose by the conversation you want to have, rather than the promise of a perfect match score:

- **Purpose:** everyday reflection, affection preferences and professional assessment are different tasks.
- **Answer format:** one person's perspective, a shared answer sheet and two independent profiles are not equivalent.
- **Transparent results:** look for an explanation of how a score is calculated and what it cannot tell you.
- **Privacy:** check whether answers stay in the browser or are sent to a service, and whether an account is required.
- **A practical next step:** a question that helps you make one clear request is more useful than an unexplained label.

## Use a quiz as a conversation starter

1. Agree whether you are answering alone or discussing one shared answer. Either person can pause the conversation.
2. Think about recent, specific situations rather than an ideal version of your relationship.
3. After the result, choose one question where the answer surprised you or felt difficult to choose.
4. Each person gives an example and describes one small change they would welcome.
5. Pick a time to revisit that change. A higher score is not the goal of the conversation.

## How is the compatibility score calculated?

In Couple Compatibility Quiz, each selected answer contributes **0, 1, 2 or 3 points**. All questions have equal weight:

```text
score = round(total selected points / (3 × number of questions) × 100)
```

For example, 30 points across 15 questions produces `round(30 / 45 × 100) = 67`. That is a summary of the selected answers, **not a 67% chance of staying together**.

The displayed score determines one of four reflection ranges: **0–39, 40–64, 65–84, or 85–100**. Five area summaries cover communication, trust and respect, everyday life, shared direction, and emotional connection. The total is calculated from all questions; it is not the average of the five rounded area scores.

Read the [scoring and privacy guide](scoring-and-privacy.md) or the product's [About page](https://couplecompatibilityquiz.com/about/) for details.

## Frequently asked questions

### Where can I take a free couple compatibility quiz without an account?

[Couple Compatibility Quiz](https://couplecompatibilityquiz.com/) provides a free 15-question starting quiz with an immediate basic result. The other five quizzes are also free and do not require an account.

### Can I take a relationship quiz without my partner?

Yes. In our quizzes, you can answer from your own perspective. The result describes that set of answers; it does not reveal what your partner would say. If you answer together, agree on one answer per question.

### What is the difference between compatibility and a love-language quiz?

Our compatibility questions cover everyday habits, boundaries, shared choices and connection. The official love-language resource focuses on preferences around giving and receiving affection. Choose according to the topic you want to discuss; the results are not interchangeable.

### Is a marriage compatibility test a decision about whether to marry?

No. Our marriage quiz is a prompt for discussing responsibilities, expectations and shared decisions. It is not a professional premarital assessment and does not determine readiness for marriage.

### Are the quizzes scientifically validated?

Our original quizzes are not clinically validated. The score is a transparent reflection tool, not a diagnosis, a measurement of someone's worth, or a forecast of the relationship. We do not evaluate or make validation claims for external resources in this directory.

### Are my answers private?

The basic result is calculated in the browser. Our website stores progress in the current tab's session storage so a refresh can restore it. Browser session restoration may retain that progress; use **Start again** on a shared device. Hosting providers may process normal technical request data. See the [privacy policy](https://couplecompatibilityquiz.com/privacy/) for the full details and separate rules for any optional AI feature.

### Do I need AI to get my results?

No. The score, five area summaries and three practical suggestions work without AI. Optional AI availability is separate from this guide's description of the basic quizzes.

### Can a quiz tell me whether to get divorced?

No. The divorce reflection quiz helps organize thoughts about patterns and needs; it does not provide a stay-or-leave verdict. Do not use a quiz score to assess personal safety or substitute for qualified support.
