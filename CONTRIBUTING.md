# Contributing

Help keep this collection useful, specific and accurate.

For a resource suggestion, include:

1. The original publisher's URL and the resource's name.
2. The question it helps someone explore.
3. Whether it is self-reflection, a shared activity or another format.
4. A primary source for claims about price, signup or privacy, and the date you checked it.
5. Any relationship you have with its publisher.

We also welcome original conversation prompts, accessibility improvements and factual corrections. Write in plain language and preserve meaningful differences between resources.

Do not submit private relationship answers, identifiable stories about another person, copied proprietary questionnaires, fabricated reviews, unsupported accuracy claims, or promotional link lists without a useful explanation. A relationship quiz should not be described as diagnosing a condition, determining personal safety, or deciding whether someone should stay or leave.

The maintainers operate Couple Compatibility Quiz, which is featured in the README. Other publishers are not implied to endorse this repository or the product.
