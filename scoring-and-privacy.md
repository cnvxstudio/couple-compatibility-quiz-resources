# Couple Compatibility Quiz: Scoring and Privacy Explained

This guide describes the basic quizzes at [couplecompatibilityquiz.com](https://couplecompatibilityquiz.com/) as reviewed on September 25, 2026. It does not describe how other sites score their tests.

## What the result measures

The result summarizes one completed answer sheet. A person can answer from their own experience, or two people can discuss and agree on the answers. The application does not compare two independent profiles or calculate a probability that a relationship will last.

Each question has four choices whose assigned values are 0–3. The display order varies, so choosing the first visible option is not a consistent way to select a particular score. Every question is required and has the same overall weight.

## The calculation

```text
maximum points = 3 × question count
overall score = round(selected points / maximum points × 100)
```

For the 15-question main quiz, the maximum is 45. A total of 30 yields 67; a total of 38 yields 84. The rounded score determines the reflection range:

| Displayed score | Range used to select the quiz's reflection |
| --- | --- |
| 0–39 | First range |
| 40–64 | Second range |
| 65–84 | Third range |
| 85–100 | Fourth range |

Each quiz has its own wording and suggestions for these ranges. Higher numbers reflect more supportive habits in the selected choices; they are not proof that a relationship is healthy or safe.

## Why the five area scores may not average to the total

The five areas are communication, trust and respect, everyday life, shared direction, and emotional connection. Each area uses only its own questions and is rounded separately. Areas can contain different numbers of questions, while the overall calculation weights every question equally.

For example, an area with three questions contributes three questions to the total; an area with two contributes two. Averaging their displayed percentages would give those areas equal weight and would not reproduce the application rule.

## Where answers go

- **Basic quiz:** the score is calculated in the browser. No account or name is required.
- **Progress:** the current tab's session storage saves answers and progress so a refresh can restore them. Different quizzes have separate progress.
- **Clearing:** Start again clears the current quiz. Session restoration may retain tab storage, so closing a tab is not a guarantee of deletion on a shared device.
- **Technical requests:** the hosting provider may process IP addresses, browser information, request paths and operational logs.
- **Optional AI:** a separate feature, with separate explicit consent and external processing described in the privacy policy. It is not required for the basic result, and this guide does not promise its availability.

Current basic quiz pages do not load advertising or third-party analytics scripts. This is not a claim that visiting the site produces zero technical data.

## Use the result constructively

Pick one question to discuss. Explain a recent example, hear the other person's perspective, and choose a small next step. Do not treat differences as evidence that either person answered incorrectly.

These are original, non-clinical quizzes. They cannot diagnose a relationship, decide whether to marry or divorce, or assess someone's safety.
